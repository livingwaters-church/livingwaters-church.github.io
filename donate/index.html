Fellowship Hall

At Living Waters Community Church, our vision is built on connection—creating environments where people can gather, grow, and experience genuine community. As our ministry continues to expand, God is opening doors for us to deepen our impact both inside and outside our walls. Our goal for the future is to establish a dedicated Fellowship Hall where every member, guest, and community partner has a welcoming space to connect, share life, and build lasting bonds of faith.

As our church family grows, the need for a dedicated space has become clear. Many people have asked for a place to gather for special events and ministry meetings, as well as a facility equipped to serve and provide meals for members and guests. If you would like to contribute directly to the Fellowship Hall special project and support the ongoing growth of Living Waters, click the button below.
