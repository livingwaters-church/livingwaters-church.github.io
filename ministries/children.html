Children's Ministry

Helping children discover God's love, grow in faith, and build friendships through engaging Bible teaching, activities, and community.

Growing Faith From The Beginning

At Living Waters Community Church, we believe children are an important part of God's family. Our children's ministry provides a safe, welcoming environment where kids can learn biblical truths, develop friendships, and experience the love of Christ.

What We Offer

  • Bible-centered lessons designed for children
  • Interactive activities and learning experiences
  • A safe and caring environment
  • Opportunities for children to serve and grow

Join Us

We invite your family to be part of our children's ministry. Come grow with us as we help children know God and His Word.
