Hope's Cry

Living Waters Community Church proudly partners with Hope's Cry, a Christ-centered ministry dedicated to ending human trafficking through prevention, restoration, and sharing the hope of Jesus Christ with vulnerable children, families, and survivors.

🏠

Restore

Hope's Cry provides safe housing, trauma-informed care, counseling, discipleship, education, and vocational opportunities that help survivors rebuild their lives with dignity and hope.

🎓

Prevent

Through education, community outreach, and partnerships with local leaders, Hope's Cry works to prevent human trafficking before it begins and protect vulnerable children from exploitation.

🤝

Empower

By equipping churches, families, and community leaders, Hope's Cry helps create environments where trafficking cannot thrive and where lasting freedom is possible through Christ.

Our Partnership

Living Waters supports Hope's Cry because we believe every person is created in the image of God and deserves to live in freedom. Through prayer, financial support, and raising awareness, we join Hope's Cry in bringing restoration and the Gospel to those affected by human trafficking.

🌍
